Game versions · Purchases · Your rights · Contact

Which version are you using?

  • Android — FerroFlect production game: 15 free levels and an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. RevenueCat manages purchase verification and Restore Purchases. The production game contains no advertisements, AdMob or advertising SDK integration. There are no subscriptions, and no RFJ Games account is required.
  • Web: no advertisements or native Google Play or RevenueCat integration. Progress and settings are stored in your browser. Netlify hosts the website.
  • Historical Android test builds only: some earlier Closed testing versions included Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The historical section below applies only if you are still using one of those older advertising-enabled builds, not to the production game.

Progress and settings on your device

The game stores completed levels, music and sound settings, and which opening and game-mechanic introductions you have seen. These records support progression and preferences. The game does not upload them to an RFJ Games server or RevenueCat.

Progress does not automatically synchronise between devices. Restoring a purchase restores eligible purchase access, not completed levels. Advertising counters and consent data from historical test builds may remain after an update; the production game does not use them.

Google Play purchases and RevenueCat

Google Play processes payments. RFJ Games does not receive your payment-card number or Google account password through the game. RevenueCat helps retrieve products and localised prices, verify purchases, restore eligible purchases and determine whether paid content is available (entitlement status).

RevenueCat normally creates a random customer identifier, called an anonymous App User ID. It can be linked to purchase history, so it is a pseudonymous identifier, not a guarantee of anonymous data. The game does not supply your name or email address as a customer attribute.

Purchase services process information such as product identifiers, purchase tokens or transaction references, purchase history and access status. RevenueCat also processes technical information such as device model, operating-system and app/SDK versions, language, currency and service request information. Network connections involve IP addresses. Purchase information is available to RFJ Games for access management, support and purchase reporting.

Connections may occur when the game starts or checks access, even before a purchase. Automatic collection of advertising identifiers through RevenueCat is disabled in this version; purchase identifiers and technical information are still processed.

The SDK caches purchase information on the device. Available, previously verified information may allow offline access, but offline purchase access is not guaranteed indefinitely. Buying, restoring and obtaining server updates require an internet connection. After reinstalling or changing devices, use Restore Purchases with the relevant Google Play account if access needs to be restored.

Android backup

Android Auto Backup is enabled. Depending on the device, Android version and your backup settings, app data may be included in system backups or device transfers. This is separate from an RFJ Games cloud-save service. Restoration of progress is not guaranteed. Manage backups through your device or account settings.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain until removed, cleared or overwritten. Clearing Android app storage or browser site data removes these local records. Uninstalling normally removes the local app copy, but backups may retain or restore data.

Uninstalling does not delete purchase records held by Google or RevenueCat. Purchase records support verification, restoration and support; retention depends on those purposes and applicable obligations. Support correspondence is kept as needed to address the request and any related obligations.

Contact us to request deletion of information we control, including relevant data processed for us by RevenueCat. We may need proportionate information to locate the correct record and verify the request. We will explain any retention requirement or limitation. Deletion does not itself refund a purchase or erase Google's independently held records. Using purchase services again may create new records. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

Age audience

FerroFlect's intended Google Play audience is ages 13–15, 16–17 and adults aged 18 or older. It is not directed at children under 13. Its PEGI 3 content rating describes the game's content; it is not the intended age audience. The game does not ask players to enter their age or create an RFJ Games account.
